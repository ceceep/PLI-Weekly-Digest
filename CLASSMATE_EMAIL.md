# Email to Classmates

## VERSION A: For Use Now (Laptop-Based)

**Subject:** PLI Weekly Digest - Automated Assignment Reminders

---

Hi everyone!

I built a tool to help us stay on top of all our PLI assignments, readings, and deadlines. It sends automated weekly digests and daily reminders so we never forget what's due.

**What you'll get if you subscribe:**
- **Weekly Digest** every Sunday at 8:00am PT with everything due in the next 7 days
- **Daily Reminder** every evening at 6:00pm PT for items due tomorrow
- All course materials (assignments, readings, sessions) in one clean email
- Tracks multiple courses automatically
- Custom reminders for things like group payments, events, etc.

**How to subscribe:**

Since I'm running this on my laptop for now, I'll need to add you manually. Just reply to this email or Slack me with:
- Your email address
- Your name (optional)

Or if you're on campus and want to sign up in person, find me and I can add you right away!

**What I need from the class:**

I've already uploaded our Education 263B syllabus, but if anyone has syllabi from other courses we're all taking (as PDFs), send them my way and I'll add them to the digest too!

**Future plans:**

If this is helpful, I can deploy it to the cloud so it runs 24/7 automatically. For now, I'll keep my laptop running to make sure everyone gets their digests on time.

Let me know if you want to be added!

Best,
Cecee
